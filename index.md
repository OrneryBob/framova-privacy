# Framova Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026

Framova is a screenshot, screen recording and video editing app for macOS made by Dylan Sola. This policy explains what Framova does with your information. The short version: your captures stay on your Mac, and Framova does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

## What Framova stores, and where

Everything Framova creates is kept locally on your Mac, inside the app's own sandboxed container:

- Screenshots, screen recordings and their markups
- Studio projects and any media you import into them
- Your settings, such as hotkeys, capture preferences and export defaults

These files never leave your Mac unless you choose to export, copy or share them yourself. Framova has no servers, no accounts, and no cloud sync. Deleting a capture in the app deletes it from your Mac, and uninstalling the app removes its container.

## Permissions Framova asks for

Framova only asks for the system permissions it needs to do its job, and only when you use the related feature:

- **Screen Recording** is required to take screenshots and record your screen. Framova captures only what you choose to capture, and only while you are capturing.
- **Microphone** is used only if you turn on "Record microphone" in Settings, and only while a recording is running. Your voice is saved into the recording file on your Mac and nowhere else.
- **Login Items** is used only if you turn on "Launch Framova at login".

You can change or revoke any of these at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

## Purchases

Framova Pro is sold through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes the payment and manages your subscription or lifetime purchase. Framova receives only a confirmation from Apple that the purchase is valid, which it uses to unlock Pro features on your Mac. Framova never sees your name, email address, payment details or Apple Account information.

Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.

## Feedback and bug reports

Settings → About and the Help menu let you send feedback or report a bug. This opens an email in your own mail app, addressed to the developer, which you can read and edit before deciding whether to send it. Besides your message, it includes the details shown in the form: the Framova version, your Mac model and macOS version, whether you're on the free or Pro plan, and whether Screen Recording and Microphone access are allowed. Your captures, recordings and projects are never included unless you attach them yourself. Nothing is sent unless you click Send, and anything you send is used only to answer you and to fix problems.

## What Framova does not do

- No analytics, usage tracking or telemetry
- No crash reporting services
- No advertising or ad identifiers
- No third-party SDKs that collect data
- No network connections, other than the App Store checking your purchase
- No access to your files beyond the ones you explicitly open, import or save through a file dialog or drag and drop

## Children

Framova does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If Framova ever changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated and the effective date above will change. The current version is always available at this page.
